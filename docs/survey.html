Metric definitions, data provenance & downloads

This new portfolio demonstration uses generated, pseudonymous survey records for August–October 2026. Completion rate = completed / usable responses. CSAT = completed responses scoring 4 or 5 / completed responses. Median time uses completed responses only. Market and channel rates are calculated from response counts, rather than averaged percentages. Quality metrics always cover the full import.
